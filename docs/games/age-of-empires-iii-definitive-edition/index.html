What MacGames does for it.

Age of Empires III: Definitive Edition (AoE3 DE) is a Windows game. MacGames brings the parts it needs to run on a Mac, and sets them up for you.

  • DirectX through Metal

    Apple's D3DMetal turns the game's DirectX calls into Metal.

  • No hardware warning

    MacGames sets the registry values that skip the game's unsupported-system check and its first-run hardware setup.

  • Its files kept apart

    The game keeps its home, cache and settings folders inside its own MacGames folder.

  • One Steam for your games

    The game installs into the shared Steam library, so you sign in to Steam once for all your games.

From Set up to Play.

  1. Set up

    Download MacGames, choose Age of Empires III: Definitive Edition and press Set up. MacGames checks your Mac and makes a Windows environment for the game.

  2. Install

    Steam opens. Sign in with your own account, then install Age of Empires III: Definitive Edition and keep the default folder.

  3. Play

    Press Play. The game starts with its renderer, its fixes and your settings.

Questions.

Do I need to buy Age of Empires III: Definitive Edition again?

No. You sign in to your own Steam account and install the copy you own. MacGames never includes or downloads games itself.

Which Macs can run it?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26 or later, with Rosetta 2. MacGames tells you how to install Rosetta 2 if it is missing. Frame rates depend on your Mac.

Has it been tested?

Not yet. Its recipe is complete, but nobody has tested it in MacGames so far. Your test report helps it reach Tested.

Can I play online?

Online play with anti-cheat is at your own risk.

Is MacGames free?

Yes. MacGames is free and open source under the PolyForm Noncommercial license. Nobody can sell it.
